RULES & FAQ

How The Challenge Works

Simple guidelines on scoring, leaderboards, and daily submission limits.

HOW SCORING WORKS

Submissions are ranked primarily by R² Score (how closely your predicted salaries track actual salaries). Closer to 1.0 is better! If two teams have the same R², the team with lower RMSE (average salary error in ₹ Lakhs) wins the tiebreaker.

PUBLIC VS PRIVATE LEADERBOARD

The 100,000 test students are split 50/50. During the competition, your score on the live leaderboard is calculated on the 50,000 Public rows. At the end of the competition, final winners are evaluated on the remaining 50,000 Private rows so nobody can cheat or overfit.

DAILY SUBMISSION LIMIT

Each team gets 5 submissions every 24 hours. Your highest score is automatically saved, so you can keep trying and improving without losing your rank!
